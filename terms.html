What you agree to when you pitch

Ticking "I consent to my work being displayed" on the pitch form means the points below. Nothing more. A pitch is not a commitment on either side.

Your rights

  • You keep ownership of your idea and your work. Pitching transfers nothing.
  • You confirm you hold the rights to what you describe, or have permission for any material by others.
  • You can withdraw at any time by emailing hello@aisafety.museum.

What we may do with your pitch

  • Read and score it. A small volunteer screening committee reads every pitch. Advisors from the AI safety field see the shortlist.
  • Contact you by email about your pitch, the shortlist, and later showings.
  • Show your work at the Berlin venues if it is selected and you complete stage two. Selection is not guaranteed.

We do not publish pitches, share them outside the screening group, or use them for anything other than selecting exhibits.

Your data

  • Your name, email, location, and pitch are stored in our submissions database, hosted on Airtable, and are visible only to the organiser and screeners.
  • The link you used to reach the form may record which channel it came from, so we know where to post next time. No other tracking.
  • We keep pitches for the duration of the exhibition programme and delete them on request. Email hello@aisafety.museum.

If you are shortlisted

Stage two comes with the full terms: your choice of license (Creative Commons or display-only), how you are credited, consent to photography of the installed work, safety requirements for physical pieces, and the rule that interactive pieces record only anonymous usage data. You decide all of that then, with the full text in front of you. The summary on the open call page is accurate.

Who you are agreeing with

The Museum for AI Safety & Society is a volunteer project. Until it has a host organisation, the counterparty is Justin Shenk as organiser. If a host organisation takes over, it will offer the same conditions, and you may decline and withdraw at that point.
