Privacy notice

How we handle your data

This notice explains what personal data the AI Safety & Society Exhibition collects, why, and what you can do about it. It covers people who pitch an exhibit, volunteer, write to us, or visit the exhibition. It is written to meet Articles 13 and 14 of the GDPR.

Who is responsible

The exhibition is a volunteer project without its own legal entity. The data controller is:

Justin Shenk
[postal address]
Berlin, Germany
hello@aisafety.museum

If a host organization takes over the exhibition, it will become the controller for data collected from then on, and we will update this notice and tell everyone whose data we hold.

What we collect and why

People who pitch an exhibit

Data
Name, email, location, category, pitch text, image or link, equipment needs, the channel tag on the link you used
Purpose
Reading and scoring pitches, contacting you about the shortlist and later showings, planning the floor
Legal basis
Steps at your request before a contract, Art. 6(1)(b); consent for optional fields, Art. 6(1)(a)
Kept until
End of the exhibition program, at most 24 months after your pitch, or on request

Selected creators

Data
The above plus setup guides, license choice, credit line, photos and video of the installed work
Purpose
Showing, documenting, and crediting the work; the open kit if you opt in
Legal basis
Contract, Art. 6(1)(b); consent for photography of you, Art. 6(1)(a)
Kept until
Documentation stays published under the license you chose; contact data 24 months after the last showing

Volunteers

Data
Name, email, location, skills, availability, what you want to work on
Purpose
Placing you in a role, inviting you to the Slack and meetings
Legal basis
Steps at your request, Art. 6(1)(b); legitimate interest in running the project, Art. 6(1)(f)
Kept until
12 months after you stop volunteering

Anyone who emails us

Data
Your email address and what you write
Purpose
Answering you
Legal basis
Legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)
Kept until
12 months

Visitors

Data
Anonymous, aggregate interaction counts on some pieces (presses, choices, session length). Photos and video of the room, which may show you
Purpose
Evaluating the pilot; documenting the exhibition
Legal basis
Anonymous data is outside the GDPR. Photos and video: legitimate interest in documenting a public event, Art. 6(1)(f), with signage and the right to object on the spot
Kept until
Aggregate figures indefinitely; footage until the project ends unless published

Website visitors

Data
Server logs held by our host (IP address, page, time). A channel tag in your browser's local storage if you arrived through a tagged link
Purpose
Serving the site; knowing which channels bring pitches
Legal basis
Legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f)
Kept until
Logs per the host's standard retention; the local tag until you clear your browser

We do not use analytics or advertising cookies. Interactive pieces never store names, free text, voice, images, or device identifiers; the submission terms forbid it.

Who else sees it

  • Screeners and advisors, all volunteers, see pitches only for selection.
  • Service providers that process data on our behalf: Airtable (submissions database), Google (the hello@aisafety.museum mailbox is read through Gmail; volunteer form and outreach records in Google Sheets), Vercel (website hosting), Slack (volunteer workspace), Open-Xchange via EuroDNS (mail hosting). Airtable, Google, Slack, and Vercel are US companies; transfers rest on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or standard contractual clauses, as each provider publishes.
  • Venues (EAGx Berlin, Foresight Institute Berlin Node, AI Safety Berlin) receive only what they need to host a piece: the creator's name and the setup guide.
  • Policymakers, journalists, and educators may see selected works in briefings, credited as the creator chose. No applicant data goes with them.

We do not sell data or share it for anyone else's marketing.

Your rights

You can ask us to show you the data we hold on you, correct it, delete it, restrict its use, or hand it over in a portable form. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time; where we rely on legitimate interest, you can object. Email hello@aisafety.museum and we answer within a month. You can also complain to the Berlin data protection authority, the Berliner Beauftragte für Datenschutz und Informationsfreiheit, at datenschutz-berlin.de.

Under 18

We do not collect data from anyone under 18. If a creator is a minor, a parent, legal guardian, or teacher submits on their behalf and is our only contact.

Changes

We will update this notice when the project gains a host organization, adds a venue, or changes a provider. The version and date are below.
